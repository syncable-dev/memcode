# Integration marks

These files identify the tools MemCode connects to. They are not MemCode branding and do not
imply sponsorship or endorsement. Marks remain owned by their holders.

- `slack.svg`: the mark of Slack's developer documentation logo,
  <https://docs.slack.dev/img/logos/logo-light.svg>, without the words beside it. Geometry and
  colors are unchanged.
