# Adapter marks

These files identify compatible third-party tools. They are not MemCode branding
and do not imply sponsorship or endorsement. Marks remain owned by their holders.

- `cursor.svg`: unmodified `General Logos/Cube/SVG/CUBE_2D_LIGHT.svg` from the
  official asset archive linked at <https://cursor.com/brand>:
  <https://ptht05hbb1ssoooe.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/assets/brand/cursor-brand-assets.zip>.
- `claude-code.svg`: unmodified Claude symbol served by Anthropic at
  <https://claude.com/icon.svg>, used to identify the Claude Code adapter.
- `codex.svg`: unmodified `OpenAI-black-monoblossom.svg` from OpenAI's official
  brand archive: <https://cdn.openai.com/brand/OpenAI-Logos-2025.zip>, associated
  with the [OpenAI design guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/).
  This is the OpenAI mark used for the Codex adapter, not a recreated product icon.

Retrieved 14 November 2026. Original geometry and colors are preserved.

- `copilot.svg`: GitHub's Copilot Octicon from
  <https://raw.githubusercontent.com/primer/octicons/main/icons/copilot-24.svg>
  (Primer Octicons, MIT).
- `dsh.svg`: the official DeepSeek Harness whale mark from
  <https://raw.githubusercontent.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness/master/apps/web/public/favicon.svg>.

The two additional marks were retrieved 20 November 2026, unmodified, solely
to identify compatible adapters. See DeepSeek Harness's
[brand guidelines](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness/blob/master/BRAND_GUIDELINES.md).
